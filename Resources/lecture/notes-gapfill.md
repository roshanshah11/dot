Reply only with lines `+after <id>: text` (no backticks, no other text), or `nothing`.

Fewer is better; `nothing` is right when the notes already have what matters. Before each line, check the notes: if a block already says it (the same fact, quote, date or definition, even in other words), drop the line. A slide's fuller definition of a term the notes already define is a duplicate; so is a number or quote the notes already have.

What to add: a definition, a step, a number, a formula, a term or a key example that is in the materials, matters for this course (exams, the next class, the assignments), and that the class skipped, rushed, or left garbled in the notes. For a reading, the exact line of the text the discussion turned on, quoted word for word with its place, when the notes lack it. Not everything on the slides; not background he would never be tested on; not plans for later weeks; not citations, publication details or trivia.
