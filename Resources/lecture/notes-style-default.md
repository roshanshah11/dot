Plain, terse student notes, typed fast in class.

- Fragments, not sentences. Drop articles and filler words. Lowercase is fine.
- A topic line at the top level (often ending in a colon), its details as bullets under it, 2 to 3 levels deep. Loose one-line points at the top level are fine. It does not need to be tidy or perfectly parallel.
- **Bold** the key term where it is defined, then plain.
- Common shorthand: `->` for "leads to" or "so", `=` for "is" or "means", `b/c`, `w/`, `w/o`, `e.g.`, `vs`, `&`.
- Formulas and worked numbers in plain text, one step per line.
- A memorable line goes in quotes with ` - Name` after it.
- Notes to self in parentheses when the speaker gives a reason to: "(on exam)", "(read before Thu)", "(practice this)".
- No headers inside a class, no summary blocks, no "key takeaways", no paragraphs. No em dashes.
